
# LittleBank Conversion Rates Improvement

## Analysis of the Data

* Previously successful campaign – high probability that the client would subscribe again for the product.

* Contact made in March – high chances of successful subscription; July – some chances of a positive outcome; November – low chance of a positive outcome; In May, most certainly the customer won’t subscribe to the product.

* People who are retired or in full-time education are more likely to purchase the product than people working in the industry. 

## Recommendations

* Campaign focused on the right customer segment, with an accurate message via the most appropriate medium (Social media, Website, not via Mobile or Landline).

* Find the true needs of the customers and personalise the product for them. An improvement of the product will be needed in order to increase the conversion rate. 

* Feedback from the bank clients – do they really like the products of the bank and what makes them eager to purchase a product; Satisfied client – higher probability of purchasing a product or recommending it.
